# What To Do If You Have Been Scammed

## Stop all payments
Do not send any more money, even if the scammer threatens to cancel the offer or promises a refund. Do not share OTPs, bank passwords or ID documents.

## Preserve evidence
Take screenshots of chats, emails, payment receipts, the offer letter, phone numbers, UPI IDs and websites. Do not delete the conversation.

## Report the fraud
Report online at cybercrime.gov.in or call the national cybercrime helpline 1930 as soon as possible. Quick reporting improves the chance of freezing the money.

## Inform your bank
Call your bank or UPI provider immediately, report the transaction and ask them to block or flag the receiving account.

## Warn others
Tell your college placement cell and friends so that others do not fall for the same offer. Report the fake profile or page on the platform where it appeared.

## Protect your identity
If you shared Aadhaar, PAN or bank details, monitor your accounts and consider informing your bank about possible misuse.

Sources: cybercrime.gov.in, helpline 1930.
