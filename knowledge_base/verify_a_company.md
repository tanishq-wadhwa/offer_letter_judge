# How to Verify a Company

## Check official registration
Search the company name on the Ministry of Corporate Affairs website (MCA21) under company master data. A real Indian company has a CIN, a registered address and a list of directors. Note the incorporation date.

## Inspect the website and domain
Visit the official website and look at the careers page. Check the domain age with a WHOIS lookup. A company website registered only a few weeks ago, while the company claims to be established, is suspicious.

## Use LinkedIn carefully
Look for the company page with a reasonable history, employee count and real employees who list the company. Search for the recruiter by name and check that they work there. A page created recently with few followers is a warning sign.

## Match the email to the website
Official HR emails use the company domain. Confirm the same domain on the company website and careers page.

## Read reviews and complaints
Search the company name with words such as scam, fraud, fake offer or reviews. Check Glassdoor, Google reviews and discussion forums. Several complaints about fees are decisive.

## Call the official number
Find the company phone number from its official website, not from the offer letter, and ask HR to confirm your offer and your name.

Sources: mca.gov.in, official company websites.
